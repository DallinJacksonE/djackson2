## Overview
My first portfolio website was not as professional as I wanted it to be. This is version 2.0 and a small synopsis.

* [Live Demo](https://djackson.dev)
* [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/DallinJacksonE/djackson2)
* [Version 1.0 Repository](https://github.com/DallinJacksonE/djackson)


## Tech Stack

* **React & TypeScript:** For building a robust, type-safe user interface.
* **Vite:** Chosen over Create React App for significantly faster compilation and hot-module replacement.
* **Docker:** Container management and rebooting to ensure live deployment.
* **Cloudflare Tunnel:** Self hosted on second hand pc to eliminate cloud computing costs.


## Process
Having a professional online presence is an important evil. I am not a fan of LinkedIn or other social medias, mostly because I lose too much time on them. Building a website where I can showcase my work, invite collaboration, and post tutorials and blog posts is much more the type of online presence I prefer. This website is an overhaul of a previous website, which I felt was nessecary to make a better implementation. The past one was not very well styled, had some performance issues, and was limited in functionality. Learning from that I was able to ensure that this site had a better foundation, less dependencies on packages, and a cleaner code structure.

I hope you have enjoyed this website as its visitor, I suppose it speaks for itself on whether I did a good job or not. I had fun rebuilding a simple static site which is a bit less complicated than the full stack websites I build during the workday.
